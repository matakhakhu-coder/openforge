OpenForge is a directory. It points to South African civic data: gazettes, tenders, municipal finance, and the tools people have built on top of them. It does not host any of that data, and it is not an official source.

That raises a fair question. If we only point to other people's work, why trust the pointer?

You shouldn't, not blindly. So every entry carries three things: a status, a confidence level, and the date we last opened the page and read it.

## What the status tells you

There are four. Unverified means the entry is listed but nobody has confirmed it. Live means it was checked and working on the date shown. Moved means the publisher has pointed visitors somewhere else. Dead means it can no longer be reached.

New entries start as unverified. They have to earn the rest.

## A worked example

Open Gazettes is a searchable archive of South African government gazettes, run by OpenUp. When we read it on 26 October 2026, the site was showing a notice recommending gazettes.africa for up to date gazettes.

So the entry is marked moved, with high confidence, and the note says why.

We could have deleted it and listed only the replacement. We kept it. If you bookmarked Open Gazettes or cited it somewhere, you land on our page and find out where things went.

Gazettes.Africa is listed as live. It describes itself as a digital collection of African gazettes, with a South Africa section, and says it holds over 158,000. That number is their claim, not ours. We have not checked it, and we have not checked the licence terms.

## Confidence is not the same as status

Status says what we found. Confidence says how sure we are of it.

The Government Printing Works is listed as live, with medium confidence. We read the homepage and saw links to eGazettes and Tenders, but we did not open them. Medium is the honest rating.

Municipal Money, from National Treasury, is live with high confidence. That rating covers the pointer, not the numbers behind it. The site shows 2026 Q2 figures and says the data is still being verified for completeness. We put that in the note so you see it before you rely on it.

## What we do not do

We do not rate the data inside a source. We rate whether the source is where we say it is, and how recently we looked.

We do not hide entries that have gone wrong. A dead link with a date on it tells you more than a missing one.

And unverified means unverified. Treat it as a lead, not a fact.

## What is next

More entries, each read before it is rated. If you find one that is out of date, tell us. A correction is worth more to this directory than a new listing.
